# House Rules (every series)

- Second-person narration ("you"). Serious and dramatic. No jokes, no comic relief, no mascot.
- Each series sets its own visual style (in its style_line.txt), and styles may differ between series. Keep visuals simple, uncluttered and readable on a phone; the narration carries the drama.
- The main character is an everyperson the viewer can imagine being: simplified, expressive but generic features, no detailed or distinctive likeness (no individual hairstyles, stubble, scars or tattoos).
- Short, fast lines.
- Every episode runs at least 60 seconds of narration; aim for about 65 seconds.
- Every story shot is a literal, physically possible scene: one sun in the sky, no symbolic counters or icons standing in for numbers or time (never "four suns" for four days).
- Educational lines may cut away from the story to a short 3D explainer shot of the fact itself, in the series style, like a documentary graphic: e.g. a simplified see-through human body with the kidneys glowing, salt crystals forming in a glass of seawater. One clear idea per shot, on a plain dark background. No text, labels, arrows or numbers in the picture. Cutaways don't show the main character.
- Open on a hook in the first line.
- End on a cliffhanger. Never a vote, a question to the audience, "comment below," or any ask for viewers to choose the path.
- When a series teaches facts, every factual claim comes from that series' sources and is listed in the claims.
- Original characters only. No copyrighted characters, no real people.
